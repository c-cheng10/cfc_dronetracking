Why: fixes the false-alarm budget on the hardest negatives, and does not depend on the
number of background boxes per drone (precision and F1 do). One global count threshold
is crude because event counts scale with box size and distance; that is the baseline's problem.

Notes on the columns:

  to the outside world. FA/min = false alarms per minute of background box-time
  (each window adds 0.25 s; per single background box).

=== NOTES (written by hand after the November 6, 2026 run; re-running the script drops this block) ===
1. The FFT does NOT beat count-only on active backgrounds overall. AUC, FFT vs count:
   heldout 0.886 vs 0.932, challenging 0.527 vs 0.797. Per type it wins only for Betafpv air75
   on heldout (0.764 vs 0.705); Mini 2 ties (0.981 vs 0.982).
2. Count matching mostly failed: in 18 of 22 tracks the active boxes have under half the drone's
   median events (act/drone 0.02-0.46); the scene has nothing as busy as a nearby drone. Where it
   came closer (train/95 0.52, train/78 0.62, train/67 0.46) the FFT edges out count
   (0.764/0.705, 0.671/0.658, 0.720/0.703). In the one matched sequence (test/111, 1.06) the
   FFT collapses (0.056) because of item 3.
3. Most active-box false alarms peak at exactly 100, 200 or 300 Hz: light flicker at harmonics of
   50 Hz mains (test/111: 705 of 1098 active windows peak at 200 Hz; train/108 and train/36 the
   same). The prominence score cannot tell a rotor from a flickering light. Not fixed here; an
   obvious next step is to reject peaks within a few Hz of 100*k Hz (tune it on the tune set only;
   the Mini 3's blade-pass near 200-230 Hz sits close to the 200 Hz line).
4. DJI Tello EDU in test/178 and train/177: FFT AUC ~0.52 even against shifted boxes, i.e. no
   rotor signal at all there; count still works (0.95).
5. Downsampling by 2 and 4 changes nothing (all AUCs within 0.005). Expected: the score sums every
   event in the box, and integer-dividing coordinates only moves box edges by under one coarse
   pixel. This simulation cannot answer "does a smaller sensor lose the rotor"; that needs a model
   of per-pixel event generation (contrast threshold, refractory merging), not coordinate binning.
6. Width: FFT recall at the frozen threshold rises with box width (0.07 below 20 px [n=31],
   0.17 at 20-30, 0.19 at 30-45, 0.31 at 45-70, 0.39 at 70-100, 0.43 at 100-150, 0.56 above 150).
   There is no sharp width where the peak vanishes; drone type matters more than width
   (Mini 2 recall 0.64 at 20-30 px, Betafpv 0.02-0.09 at every width it appears).
